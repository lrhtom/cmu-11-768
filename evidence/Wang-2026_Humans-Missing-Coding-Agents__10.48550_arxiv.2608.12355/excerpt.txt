【claim 1】（第 1 节）研究盯着自主完成任务、把基准难度当价值；真实编程是反复交互、部分委派、目标会变、持续监督；可能在收益递减方向上使劲；人机交互不是给不成熟模型的临时补丁，能一起摸索出新工作方式。
p.1 Position："AI coding agent research is fixated on autonomous task completion, treating benchmark difficulty as a proxy for practical value."
p.2："Yet real-world programming is rarely a one-shot activity. Instead, it unfolds through iterative interaction, partial delegation, evolving goals, and continuous human oversight." "human-centered interaction is not a temporary workaround for immature models: it enables new forms of collaboration in which agents and users jointly discover emerging workflows."
p.4："the field may be optimizing in a direction of diminishing returns, pursuing ever-harder tasks while marginal utility gains shrink"
p.3 §2：更难的基准、复杂框架、训练环境三个方向。
